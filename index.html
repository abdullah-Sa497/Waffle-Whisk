THE WAFFLE WHISK PROMISE

No cold waffles. No 30-minute chai.

Breakfast out in Lahore can mean slow service, cold plates and squeezed-in seats. We built the opposite.

  • Hot off the iron.

    Every waffle is made the moment you order, so it reaches you crisp and hot. Never soggy, never sitting.

  • Your chai comes first.

    Drinks land within minutes of sitting down. Nobody should wait half an hour for a cup of tea.

    Big tables, comfy corners and seats that are not squeezed together. Book ahead and your table is ready.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions before you come over?

Do I need to book?

Walk-ins are always welcome. Weekends get busy after 10 AM, so booking or joining the waitlist saves you the wait.

Is there parking?

Yes, there is parking right outside on MM Alam Road. On busy weekend mornings, arriving a little early helps.

Is it expensive?

Most plates cost between Rs 950 and Rs 1,700, and plenty of them are big enough to share. Students get 20% off Monday to Thursday.

Is everything halal?

Yes. Every single thing on the menu is 100% halal.

Can I bring my kids?

Please do. We have high chairs and plenty of room for strollers.

Do you deliver?

Yes. Order through Foodpanda, or message us on WhatsApp and pick it up hot.
